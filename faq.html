FAQs

Got Questions?

Here's everything we get asked most. Can't find what you need? Get in touch and we'll help directly.

What actually is Poker Hunters?

It's a fresh take on the treasure hunts you may remember from your youth. Your team solves clues which reveal locations where there will be a hidden playing card. By the final checkpoint you'll have collected various cards — whoever's built the best poker hand wins the day.

Do we need to know how to play poker?

Absolutely not. The poker theme works as a method of scoring really, and allows us to use playing cards at the clue locations - they're small and easier to hide ;) We'll provide you with a scoring rank sheet so you can see how well you're doing during the hunt, but there's no need to be an expert card shark!

How many people can take part?

Each car takes 2 to 5 people. There must be one designated driver, and one named navigator for the event. The event is limited to 12 teams/cars.

How much does it cost?

£25 per car, per event. Pre-payment upon booking is required, payable instantly via Monzo on the sign-up page, with full refunds given for cancellations 48hrs prior to the event.

Are the clues hard to solve?

Hmmm... Most are picture clues, with screenshots taken from Google Maps. Of course, we might hide the road names on a few - we don't want it to be too easy. But it's at a level where everyone can enjoy themselves.

Do we have to answer all the clues?

No, although we'll be impressed if you do! It's actually impossible to get to every location in the time allowed so your job is to answer a few clues, plan a provisional route, and start hunting. You only need to find five cards to make up a poker hand but the more you find the better chance you have of making a better hand. Of course, if you find four aces and a King straight off, it's pretty safe to say your job is done!

Do we have to get out of the car to answer the clues?

Yes, you do. Every card is hidden from view and some exploration at each location will be required to find them. For some, you'll even have to park up and walk as they won't be accessible by car. Sometimes, that's where the valuable cards are. And sometimes it isn't!

Where does the hunt start?

Hunts currently launch from Pistons & Rings in Northamptonshire. See Where You'll Find Us for directions and a look at the venue.

Do we need to book in advance?

Yes — Our events our quite popular so you will need to sign up and prepay before the event. Head to our sign-up page and let us know your preferred date and how many of you are coming. We'll get back to you to confirm.

What do we need to bring?

Just your car, your crew, and a smartphone with WhatsApp installed. We have a limited number of Ordnance Survey maps of the area available to hire on the day for £10, but they're not a requirement. With the advent of smartphones and Google Maps, you need nothing else, but some find convential maps easier to see the bigger picture. Obviously, if you have more people with more devices, that will help but if you've read this far you deserve that little tidbit!

Why do we need a smartphone with WhatsApp?

All the event admin is done via WhatsApp. When you arive at a checkpoint and find a card, you take a photo of it and send it to event control. The cards do not leave the location so another team are able to find the same card. We also use WhatsApp as a broadcast tool to all teams for in-event announcements. All this is the job of the Navigator in the team

Do we need additional insurance?

No. The cover you have on your car will be suitable for the event. We have an average speed limit for the event of 30mph. It's not a race, and driving fast has no bearing on the result whatsoever.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes — it's designed to be easy to pick up and genuinely fun for a family outing, a get-together with mates, or anyone after a few hours of clue-cracking fun.

Is it a race? Do we have to rush around?

Absolutely not. The event lasts for three and a half hours from the time you are given your clues, and you are only allowed to cover 105 miles as a maximum, with no location is more than 20 miles from the starting point. The trick is to solve the clues and pick the most efficent route you can to give you enough time to get back. If you exceed this mileage cap, you will be disqualified, and that sucks. It goes without saying that safety is paramount, ALL laws and byelaws must be obeyed at all times.

What areas do you cover?

Right now, hunts run across Northamptonshire, launching from Pistons & Rings. We do run custom events further afield so, if you're interested, please get in touch and we'll let you know if we can help.
