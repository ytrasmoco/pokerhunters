Our Story

A new kind of day out, built for glove-box adventurers

Poker Hunters launched in 2025 with a simple idea: to revive the fun of the 1980s, get people off the sofa and into the car for a few hours of clue-cracking, card-collecting fun. No experience needed. just a route, a deck of cards, and a car full of people who fancy a laugh.

Whether it's a family outing, a get-together with mates, or an excuse to explore a corner of the county you've never been to, every hunt is designed to be easy to pick up and genuinely fun to play — right through to the last card.

How It Works

Four steps to your best hand

Every Poker Hunters event follows the same simple format, wherever in the county you're playing.

01

Gather your crew

Round up 2 to 5 people and pile into one car — that's your team for the day.

02

Follow the clues

Solve a number of clues that will lead you to hidden checkpoints across the local area.

03

Collect your cards

Find a random playing card waiting at each checkpoint and add it to your growing hand.

04

Did you win?

Reach the final checkpoint, compare with other teams. Do your cards add up to the best hand of the day?
